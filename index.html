FAQ

Questions people ask us

Is Alpha Coders a government office or an authorized Aadhaar centre?

No. Alpha Coders is a private digital services business. It is not a government office and is not an authorized Aadhaar centre.

How does Aadhaar address update assistance work?

We help you only through authorized UIDAI channels. We cannot change Aadhaar records ourselves. Message us on WhatsApp to ask what you need to get started.

What will it cost?

Official government fees, where they apply, are set by the relevant department, board or organisation. Alpha Coders service charges are separate and are for our assistance.

We do not list prices on this website. Message us on WhatsApp to ask about the charge for your task.

Can you promise my application will be accepted?

No. Decisions are made by the concerned government departments, boards, colleges and organisations, not by Alpha Coders. We help with the process, but we cannot promise any outcome.

How do I contact you?

What should I put in my first message?

Can you help with the form I need?
